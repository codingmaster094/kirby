Concerns:

- 
  title: Belly fat
  keywords: belly fat, tummy, stomach fat, love handles, lower belly, abs fat, lose belly
  summary: Reduce belly fat with a mix of core work, full-body moves, and steady cardio — consistency matters more than crunch volume.
  focus: Core strength, daily walks or light cardio, and controlled eating habits alongside training.
  exercises:
    -
      name: Dead Bug
      how: Lie on your back, arms up, knees bent at 90°. Slowly lower opposite arm and leg toward the floor without arching your lower back. Return and switch sides.
      sets: 3 sets × 10 reps/side
      tip: Keep your lower back gently pressed into the floor.
    -
      name: Plank
      how: Forearms and toes on the floor, body in a straight line from head to heels. Brace your core and breathe steadily.
      sets: 3 rounds × 20–40 seconds
      tip: Don’t let your hips sag or pike up.
    -
      name: Mountain Climbers
      how: From a high plank, drive one knee toward your chest, then quickly switch legs in a running motion.
      sets: 3 rounds × 30 seconds
      tip: Keep shoulders over wrists and move with control.
    -
      name: Bodyweight Squats
      how: Feet shoulder-width, sit hips back and down, then stand tall. Keep chest up and knees tracking over toes.
      sets: 3 sets × 12–15 reps
      tip: Full-body moves help burn more total calories than abs-only work.
- 
  title: Lower back pain
  keywords: back pain, lower back, backache, spine, sore back, lumbar
  summary: Build a stronger core and glutes, and move gently. Stop any exercise that increases sharp pain.
  focus: Core bracing, glute activation, and mobility — avoid heavy twisting while flare-ups are active.
  exercises:
    -
      name: Glute Bridge
      how: Lie on your back, knees bent, feet flat. Squeeze glutes and lift hips until shoulders, hips, and knees align. Lower slowly.
      sets: 3 sets × 12 reps
      tip: Push through your heels, not your lower back.
    -
      name: Bird Dog
      how: On all fours, extend opposite arm and leg. Hold briefly, then switch. Keep hips level.
      sets: 3 sets × 8 reps/side
      tip: Move slow — balance and control matter more than speed.
    -
      name: Cat-Cow Mobility
      how: On all fours, alternate arching and rounding your spine with your breath.
      sets: 2 rounds × 8 slow reps
      tip: Stay in a comfortable range; no forcing.
- 
  title: Knee pain
  keywords: knee pain, knees, sore knees, joint pain knees, runner knee
  summary: Strengthen the muscles that support your knees and keep impact controlled.
  focus: Quads, glutes, and hip control. Prefer smooth, pain-free ranges of motion.
  exercises:
    -
      name: Sit-to-Stand
      how: From a chair, stand up without using your hands if possible, then sit slowly with control.
      sets: 3 sets × 10 reps
      tip: Keep knees tracking over mid-foot.
    -
      name: Side-Lying Leg Lift
      how: Lie on your side, lift the top leg about 30–45°, then lower with control.
      sets: 3 sets × 12 reps/side
      tip: Strengthens hips that help protect the knee.
    -
      name: Step-Ups (low step)
      how: Step onto a low sturdy step, drive through the working leg, then step down slowly.
      sets: 3 sets × 8 reps/side
      tip: Use a low height first; stop if pain increases.
- 
  title: Build arm strength
  keywords: arms, biceps, arm strength, weak arms, upper body, push ups
  summary: Progressive pushing and pulling builds stronger arms and upper body.
  focus: Push and pull balance, full range of motion, and gradual progress.
  exercises:
    -
      name: Push-Ups (or Knee Push-Ups)
      how: Hands under shoulders, lower chest toward the floor, then press up. Drop to knees if needed.
      sets: 3 sets × 6–12 reps
      tip: Keep a straight line from head to heels (or knees).
    -
      name: Superman Pull
      how: Lie face down, lift chest slightly, pull elbows back as if rowing, squeeze shoulder blades, then extend arms forward again.
      sets: 3 sets × 10 reps
      tip: Small controlled lifts — don’t yank the lower back.
    -
      name: Chair Dips
      how: Hands on a stable chair edge, feet forward, bend elbows to lower, then press up.
      sets: 3 sets × 8–12 reps
      tip: Keep shoulders down and away from your ears.
- 
  title: Improve stamina
  keywords: stamina, endurance, cardio, running fitness, get fitter, energy, breathlessness
  summary: Build aerobic fitness with easy sustainable sessions before adding intensity.
  focus: Consistent easy cardio, nasal breathing when possible, and gradual weekly increases.
  exercises:
    -
      name: Brisk Walk Intervals
      how: Walk easy for 2 minutes, then brisk for 1 minute. Repeat. Keep conversation possible on easy parts.
      sets: 15–25 minutes total
      tip: Consistency beats one hard session.
    -
      name: Jumping Jacks
      how: Jump feet out while raising arms overhead, then return to start.
      sets: 3 rounds × 30 seconds
      tip: Land softly; reduce range if joints feel stressed.
    -
      name: High-Knee March
      how: March in place, driving knees up and swinging arms. Progress to a light skip when ready.
      sets: 3 rounds × 30–45 seconds
      tip: Stay tall and breathe in a steady rhythm.
